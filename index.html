About

I build autonomous robots that have to work reliably around people, not only in simulation. At Enchanted Tools in Paris, I work in Navigation R&D on Mirokaï robots, across human-aware perception, navigation, multi-robot integration, and validation on physical hardware. My day-to-day work is mainly C++, Python, ROS 2, Nav2, Linux, and NVIDIA Jetson platforms.

Alongside my work, I am completing an MSc in Embedded Systems Engineering at ENSTA - Institut Polytechnique de Paris. I use personal and team projects to explore vision-language models for social navigation, humanoid robotics, embedded systems, and autonomous competition robots.

Engineering at Enchanted Tools

My work focuses on the navigation stack used by Mirokaï robots, with an emphasis on behavior that remains robust when perception, people, hardware, and deployment constraints meet in the real world.

Human-aware perception and social navigation

Integrated a complete social-navigation chain combining 3D person perception, body-orientation estimation, social-formation tracking, and a Nav2 local-costmap layer for personal space, group formations, and motion-aware proxemic costs. The work spans C++ perception and navigation components, TF integration, cost modeling, automated tests, simulation, and robot validation.

360-degree near-field obstacle perception

Reworked the near-field perception pipeline around nine time-of-flight sensors and an RGB-D camera, including synchronization, filtering, hysteresis, obstacle enrichment, depth processing, and sensor fusion. The resulting obstacle representation feeds local costmaps and collision monitoring for robust navigation close to the robot.

Multi-robot fleet orchestration

Built Open-RMF support that bridges fleet-level missions with the onboard navigation stack, then validated end-to-end orchestration and navigation across multiple physical Mirokaï robots in indoor environments.

From integration to Navigation R&D

I first joined Enchanted Tools in 2024 as a Test and Integration Technician Intern, where I designed and iterated a 3D-printed charging interface for autonomous docking and characterized robotic hands through endurance, precision, motion-capture, and field tests.

Selected Robotics Projects

VLM2Nav

Vision-Language Models for Social Robot Navigation

2026 - Present

Experimental ROS 2 stack exploring a local vision-language model as a high-level reasoning layer while conventional robotics remains responsible for motion. Temporal RGB observations become structured social decisions with freshness checks and hysteresis before semantic trajectory scoring and Nav2/MPPI execution. Qwen3-VL runs through vLLM on Jetson AGX Orin, with HuNavSim reactive pedestrians and a reproducible nine-scenario benchmark.

ROS 2 · Nav2 · MPPI · Qwen3-VL · vLLM · Jetson AGX Orin · HuNavSim

PHARE

Autonomous Humanoid Research and Experimentation Platform

2026 - Present

Co-Founder and Software & Embedded Systems Lead for a modular wheeled-biped humanoid research platform at ENSTA. The program covers ROS 2, embedded firmware and electronics, CAN architecture, state estimation, optimal control, reinforcement learning, simulation, and the transition from digital models to a physical robot.
