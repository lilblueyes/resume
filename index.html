About Me

I am a Robotics Software Engineer at Enchanted Tools in Paris, working on navigation and autonomy for Mirokaï robots. My work focuses on social navigation, human-aware perception, multi-sensor obstacle representation, and fleet orchestration on physical robots.

I am also completing an MSc in Embedded Systems Engineering at ENSTA - Institut Polytechnique de Paris. My projects span vision-language models for social navigation, humanoid robotics, and autonomous competition robots.
